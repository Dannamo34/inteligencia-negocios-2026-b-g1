# Week 02 - Business Intelligence

## KPI Definition

### KPI: Low Academic Performance Rate

The KPI measures the percentage of students who have low academic performance during an academic period.

### Formula

**Low Academic Performance Rate = (Number of students with low academic performance / Total number of enrolled students) × 100**

### Goal

**Target: ≤ 10%**

The goal is to keep the percentage of students with low academic performance at or below 10% during each academic period.

---

## Decision Enabled by the KPI

This KPI would help the university identify when the percentage of students with low academic performance exceeds the established target.

If the KPI is above 10%, the university could investigate the causes and implement actions such as academic tutoring, additional support for difficult subjects, monitoring of at-risk students, or changes in academic strategies.

This indicator would therefore support academic decision-making and help the institution take preventive actions before students fail multiple subjects or abandon their studies.

---

## Data → Information → Knowledge

### Data

The raw data would include:

- Student identification.
- Academic program.
- Courses enrolled.
- Grades obtained.
- Academic period.
- Number of failed courses.
- Number of enrolled students.

For example:

**Student:** 001  
**Course:** Mathematics  
**Grade:** 2.5  
**Academic period:** 2026-1

---

### Information

The data is processed and organized to calculate the percentage of students with low academic performance.

For example:

**Total enrolled students:** 500  
**Students with low academic performance:** 40  

**Low Academic Performance Rate = (40 / 500) × 100 = 8%**

The result shows that 8% of students have low academic performance.

---

### Knowledge

The university can interpret the information and understand that the current rate of low academic performance is 8%, which is below the established target of 10%.

However, if the rate increases to 15%, the institution could conclude that there may be an academic problem that requires investigation and intervention.

This knowledge allows decision-makers to take actions such as creating tutoring programs, identifying subjects with high failure rates, and providing additional support to students at academic risk.

---

## Type of Analytics

### Descriptive Analytics

This KPI mainly involves **descriptive analytics** because it summarizes historical academic data to show what is happening with student performance.

The dashboard could answer questions such as:

- What percentage of students have low academic performance?
- Which academic periods have the highest rate?
- Which courses have the highest number of failed students?
- How does academic performance change over time?

The KPI does not primarily predict what will happen in the future. Instead, it describes the current or historical situation and helps decision-makers understand the academic performance of students.
